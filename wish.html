My Prayer For You

Heavenly Father,

Thank You for giving me the privilege of loving such a wonderful woman. Today, on her birthday, I lift her up to You with a grateful heart.

Please surround her with Your peace, protect her wherever she goes, strengthen her when she grows tired, and remind her every day how deeply she is loved—not only by me, but most importantly by You.

Bless the work of her hands, the dreams she carries, and every prayer she quietly keeps in her heart. May every sacrifice she makes today become a testimony of Your faithfulness tomorrow.

Help me become the man You want me to be—someone who will continue to love, respect, support, and protect her through every season of life.

May our relationship always place You at the center, so that whatever challenges come our way, we will continue to grow together with faith, patience, and love.

Thank You for every memory we've created and for the many more that are still waiting for us.

I entrust her life into Your loving hands.

In Jesus' Name,
Amen.

One Last Gift

Before this journey comes to an end, there's one last gift I'd like to give you.

Not something wrapped in beautiful paper.
Not flowers that will eventually fade.
Not something expensive that time can take away.

This gift is a prayer from my heart, a promise for our future, and a reminder that no matter what life brings us, you will never walk alone.

Every memory we've shared, every challenge we've overcome, every laugh, every tear, and every dream has led us to this moment.

So before this website reaches its final page, allow me to leave these words with you.

I hope you'll carry them not only today, on your birthday, but every day that follows.

This is for you, my love.
